WHY WILSON IS NOT THE PRIMARY INTERVAL — exact coverage, computed not quoted
==============================================================================

The withdrawn argument was:
    Var(X) = 0.25b + P(X=1) - p^2 = p(1-p) - 0.25b <= p(1-p)
so a binomial interval "can only be too wide". The identity is TRUE. The
coverage conclusion DOES NOT FOLLOW: a variance inequality is not a coverage
statement, and Wilson's coverage oscillates below nominal by itself.
