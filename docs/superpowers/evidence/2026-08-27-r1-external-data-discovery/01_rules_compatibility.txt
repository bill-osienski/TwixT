CORRECTED: WHAT A CROSSING PP LINK ACTUALLY DOES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An earlier draft said a self-crossing PP link "is placed but simply does not
connect". THAT IS WRONG, and the profile I had already fetched says so: a
crossing own link CAN form part of a winning path, which may loop across
itself. The draft took a search snippet over the primary source.

The gap is therefore LARGER than stated, not smaller:
  under PP    the bridge FORMS and CONNECTS; paths may self-loop
  under ours  the bridge NEVER FORMS at all
The same move sequence yields different bridge sets and different connectivity,
so PP records contain positions our engine cannot reach. The conclusion of the
earlier draft survives; its explanation did not.

A DOCUMENTATION CONFLICT THE EARLIER DRAFT MISSED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SGF PP profile (older): "Links are not removed"
  docs.littlegolem.net (current): "You may remove your own links as part of a
                                   move to rearrange your network."
These CONFLICT on link removal. The current documentation also offers THREE
board sizes -- 24x24, 30x30, 48x48 -- and confirms the swap move is in use.

CONSEQUENCE FOR PROVENANCE. An earlier draft credited Little Golem with GOOD
provenance because SGF tags each game RU[PP]. That credit is WITHDRAWN: if the
site's own current rules text contradicts the profile that RU[PP] points to,
the tag does not pin the semantics of the recorded game. Provenance is WEAKER
than the earlier draft allowed, not stronger.

CONCLUSION. Our configuration matches no named ruleset, and the nearest one is
documented inconsistently. Any use of such a corpus would require a semantic
re-interpretation, not a format conversion.
