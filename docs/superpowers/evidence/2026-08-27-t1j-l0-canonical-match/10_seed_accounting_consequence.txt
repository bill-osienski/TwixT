WHAT I HAVE NOT DONE, AND WHY
-----------------------------
I have NOT rewritten them. Whether a spent one-shot should make its own
wiring tests unrunnable is a design question, and the same question arose
when the E4 canonical screen spent its block. There it was settled by an
explicit ruling -- 'spent' stops EXECUTION, not parsing/verification/
classification/replay -- and the fix was a structural/executable split, not
a rewrite of the tests to match whichever answer I preferred.

The same split already protects the READ path here, and it works:
  * load_l0_plan still loads the 64 tasks
  * the digest still binds
  * match_report reclassifies the run to the IDENTICAL verdict
What has legitimately changed is the EXECUTABLE half, and these 25 tests
assert the executable half. Deciding how they should read after a one-shot
completes is yours, not mine.

The seeds are spent either way. That record stands.
