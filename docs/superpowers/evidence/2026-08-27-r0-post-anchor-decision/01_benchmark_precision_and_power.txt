R0 — PAIRED BENCHMARK: PRECISION AND POWER
==============================================================================

DESIGN. Two checkpoints on the SAME opening/colour cells, fresh disjoint seeds.
Statistic: mean per-cell difference, bounded in [-1, +1]. Method: Hoeffding, the
interval this programme committed to in l0_match_rules -- distribution-free,
valid for independent bounded outcomes.

TWO DIFFERENT NUMBERS, AND THE MEMO PREVIOUSLY CONFLATED THEM:
  PRECISION  = CI half-width at n. What the interval will look like.
  DETECTION  = smallest TRUE difference found with probability >= 80%.
Detection is strictly larger. alpha = 0.05, power = 80%.

COST, MEASURED not assumed: 28.61 s/game (L0 run, 1831 s / 64 games).
Each PAIR costs TWO games. Native parent match ~60 s/game; the
anchor-referenced benchmark is cheaper because only one side pays 400 sims.

C. THE CORRECTION, STATED PLAINLY
At 48 GPU-hours the Hoeffding PRECISION is 0.0494. The earlier memo called
that the "detectable difference". It is not: the 80%-power threshold at the same
n is 0.0821, about 1.66x larger.

D. WHAT THIS CALCULATION DOES AND DOES NOT DO
It PRICES FUTURE PRECISION for a comparison that has not been designed yet. It
does NOT reject any candidate. No plausible positive effect size has ever been
measured in this stack -- cont5, warm5 and fp6 are DESCRIPTIVE results from
different mechanisms, protocols, seeds and evaluation intervals, and the ledger
states they are NOT a paired causal estimate. Their negative magnitudes cannot be
turned into a prior for the positive effect of a capacity or data change.

The candidates below are rejected on their own merits, not by this table.
