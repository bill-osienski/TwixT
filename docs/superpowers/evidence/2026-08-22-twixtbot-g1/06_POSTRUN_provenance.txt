=== 3. KNOWN GAP IN THE RUN-TIME EVIDENCE =====================================

g1_probe.py RECORDS shapes and finiteness but never ASSERTS them, so its exit 0
means "the process completed", not "the G1 criteria held". It would have exited 0
on non-finite values or a wrong movelogits shape. The run's recorded values do
satisfy every criterion, but that is established by
07_POSTRUN_verify_criteria.py — which asserts them against the recorded outputs
and proves it binds via a negative self-test — NOT by the exit code.

The probe was deliberately NOT edited: it is the exact script that ran and its
hash is in MANIFEST.sha256. Rewriting it would falsify the record. The lesson
belongs to G2 and G3: a gate probe must assert its own pass criteria.
