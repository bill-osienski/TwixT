scripts/GPU/alphazero/self_play.py:1090 from the search's root visit counts,
held in ReplayBuffer (trainer.py:1793) and consumed by train_step.

CONCLUSION: no game corpus on disk carries the MCTS visit distribution.
A capacity probe on the existing replays therefore cannot use the AlphaZero
policy target pi. It can use the terminal outcome, and the move the search
actually played -- a temperature sample from that distribution.
