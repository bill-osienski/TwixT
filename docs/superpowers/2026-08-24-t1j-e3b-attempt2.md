## 2 — History and legal sets are bound

The helper now emits, per ply, **T1j's ordered history read back through its own
`Match.getMoveX(i)` / `getMoveY(i)`** accessors, and **the full legal-cell set from
`Board.pinAllowed` for the player to move**. Both are compared after every ply, and both have
negative controls.

> **Stated limitation.** Our `TwixtState` keeps no ordered history — only `pegs`, `bridges`, `ply`.
> So this binds **T1j's own record against the sequence both engines were given**. It is not a
> cross-check of two independently maintained histories, and is not described as one. What it does
> close is attempt 1's actual defect: the compared value is now read out of T1j rather than echoed
> from the harness's arguments.

- **Coverage beyond these fixtures** — three positions, 58 plies, 21 cap combinations. No draw or
  board-full path, no swap, no non-24×24 board.
- **Absolute placement** — the E0 caveat is untouched. T1j is uncalibrated, so even a fully
  qualified adapter yields an **ordering**, not a placement.
